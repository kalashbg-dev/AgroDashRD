- **S8 (M1/M2, unit drift and wrong-unit level)**: audit M1: "La etiqueta de unidad cambia pero el precio no (presentación nominal). Mayorista 261 filas, minorista 148. Unidad canónica por producto y período; ignorar cambios de etiqueta que no mueven el precio. No convertir por tamaño a ciegas." M2 folds in: unidades mal puestas (Zapote, Sandía, Repollo) — usar el nivel de precio del producto para decidir la unidad y mandar los casos dudosos a cuarentena. User decision (2026-10-24): "8% drift + banda 4x nivel (recomendada)". Rules: (1) drift nominal — same product and same base unit, compared with the previous week's row per market; when the price reconverted with the PREVIOUS unit stays within ±8% of the previous price, the row stores with the previous unit; the amount is never re-scaled (no convertir por tamaño a ciegas); the drift rule decides first, and the level band only judges rows that kept their own label. (2) unreadable labels (e.g. "Saco/1N/DN/D lb", the G5-style corruption that survives) recover the previous week's unit under the same 8% stability rule; blank or N/D unit text keeps today's UNKNOWN_UNIT quarantine. (3) level band (M2) — the converted per-base price must stay inside [¼x, 4x] of the product's committed average level for that market and price type; outside it, the row quarantines as unit_mismatch with the level in the detail. Checks: repair + service tests, a full-file scan reconciling counts against the audit's 261/148, and a real-data reload diff against current behavior.
- **S6 (G1, price column slid one row)**: whole wholesale weeks whose price column carries neighboring weeks' row-above prices (each product leads the price of the one below; Papa at RD$8.000, Yautía at RD$48). - **S7 (G7 + S5, renames and name structure)**: user's domain model (L7, L17): the words inside a product name are properties of the product itself — a qualifier (primera/segunda, grande/mediano/pequeño) or a class in parentheses (Barahonero vs FHIA; verde/maduro) — and sizes/classes with their own prices are DISTINCT products; only spelling noise unifies. Decisions (L17, L18): spelling variants with the same words merge automatically (approved); the known erratas and the "dropped primera" renames are merged only after a report the user approves (families' suggested list); cuts that change a size word never merge.
